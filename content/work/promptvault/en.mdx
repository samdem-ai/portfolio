## The problem

Prompt-injection work is iterative: you tweak a prompt, try it on three models, tweak it again. In a chat window, the history of what you tried and what it did disappears.

## What I did

1. Challenge-first organisation: prompts live under the challenge they target, not in a flat list.
2. Every edit creates a new version, and every run is logged per model as bypassed, partial or blocked.
3. Importers for ChatGPT and Claude exports, raw JSON and plain text; export to JSON or a Markdown report.
4. Everything is stored in IndexedDB. Nothing leaves the machine.

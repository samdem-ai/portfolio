## The problem

Generic nutrition databases barely cover Algerian dishes or local packaged products, so tracking a normal day of eating here means guessing.

## What I did

1. An Expo app with a daily journal, hydration, weight and BMI tracking, streaks and achievements.
2. A Spring Boot backend (jOOQ, MySQL, Flyway migrations, JWT, RabbitMQ).
3. A FastAPI microservice calls NVIDIA NIM vision and text models for the photo scan, meal plans and chat.
4. French, English and Arabic, with a proper RTL layout.

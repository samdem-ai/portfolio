## The problem

Turning an image into a buildable mosaic is easy to fake and hard to do well: naive colour matching produces muddy results, and a pretty picture is useless without a parts list.

## What I did

1. The cropped cover is area-averaged down to a stud grid, one cell per piece.
2. Each cell is matched to the nearest real LEGO colour with CIEDE2000 in LAB space, with optional Floyd–Steinberg dithering.
3. The maths runs in a Web Worker so the UI never stutters; tiles render from a cached sprite canvas.
4. Exports: PNG, a CSV bill of materials, a paint-by-numbers PDF guide, and a permalink that restores your settings.
